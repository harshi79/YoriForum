# 🏆 Contest kit: the $0 engagement engine

*Staff playbook for 🎉 Events & Giveaways. PHCorner's Contest section has 214K+ posts — contests are how forums stay alive. All prizes below cost $0.*

## Cadence (start small, grow)
- **Monthly anchor:** Top Contributor of the Month (runs itself once set up).
- **Biweekly fun:** rotate one small contest (tutorial, giveaway, revival, bug hunt).
- Announce in Announcements + Events; winners get a winner thread + badges + coins.

## Contest templates (copy-paste + adjust dates)

### ① Top Contributor of the Month (the anchor 🏆)
- **Metric (Admin-visible):** most posts + Best Answers + likes received in the month.
- **Prize:** 🏆 Top Monthly badge + 500 YC + custom title for a month + Lounge spotlight.
- **Rules:** no spam (mods disqualify farmers), staff excluded, winners announced by the 5th.

### ② Best Tutorial (grow the knowledge base 📚)
- Entries: threads in Tutorials posted during the month. Community likes = votes (50%) + staff picks (50%).
- **Prize:** FrontPage feature + 💡 Helper III badge + 300 YC + "Author of the Month" title.

### ③ Giveaway Raffle (hype 🎁)
- Prize ideas ($0): VIP status for a month, custom title, 500 YC, featured thread, TG/Discord shoutout.
- Entry: reply to the thread (one entry per human — alts = ban from all future events).
- Draw: random (random.org screenshot posted) on the announced date, winner thread after.

### ④ Revival Race (fight dead links 🔧)
- Find dead-link threads, post working mirrors/updates; most quality revivals wins.
- **Prize:** 🔧 Fixer badge (create it!) + 300 YC.

### ⑤ Bug Hunter (free QA 🐛)
- Report real forum bugs (with steps + screenshots) in Suggestions with `[BUG]`.
- Valid unique bugs = 50 YC each + 🐛 badge; best report of month gets VIP week.

## Standard rules block (paste into every contest thread)
- One entry/account per human; alts = disqualified + suspended.
- Staff decisions are final; prizes awarded within 7 days of winners post.
- Winners posted publicly; prizes: badges + YC + titles + VIP time (no cash).
- Contest void if fewer than 5 entries (we'll roll it to next round).
