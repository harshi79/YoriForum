## 4. 📚 Tutorials & Guides — "How to ask a good question (get answers 10x faster) 💡"
*Doubles as a mini-guide. Link it whenever someone posts "help plz".*

1. **Clear title** — "Login button broken on mobile" beats "help plz".
2. **Right section** — tech problem → Help & Support.
3. **Details** — what you tried, what happened, screenshots/error text.
4. **One problem per thread** — easier to solve, easier to search.
5. **Mark solved** ✅ — when fixed, mark the best reply so others find it.

Follow this and the community will fight to help you. Promise.
