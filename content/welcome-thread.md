# 👋 Welcome to YoriForum — start here!

Hey, welcome in! ⚡ YoriForum is a community for tech talk, questions, and showing off what you build. Whether you're here to learn, help, or just chill — there's a seat for you.

## 🚀 Quick start (2 minutes)
1. **Say hi** — reply below with an intro (who you are, what you're into).
2. **Read the rules** — [/rules](/rules) (short, promise) + [FAQ](/faq).
3. **Turn on dark mode** 🌙 — avatar menu → Night Mode. You know you want to.
4. **Join a discussion** — or start one. Stuck? Post in **Help & Support**.
5. **React + upvote** good posts — that's how contributors rank up.
6. **Share anything** — files, links, tools, finds → dump them in the 📤 **Share Zone**. This forum is built for sharing (download at your own risk, scan everything ⚠️).

## 🏆 How ranks work
Post stuff, get upvotes and reactions, earn points, climb automatically:

| Rank | Points needed |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Newbie | 0 (welcome!) |
| 👤 Member | 50 |
| 💬 Regular | 200 |
| 🔥 Veteran | 500 |
| 💎 Elite | 1,000 |
| 👑 Legend | 2,500 |

Quality beats quantity — one helpful answer earns more love than ten "nice bro" replies. Farming reactions with alts = reset + suspension. Don't.

## 💡 How to get the best answers
- Post in the **right section** (tech question → Help & Support, not Off-Topic).
- Write clear titles: "Login button broken on mobile" ✅ / "help plz" ❌.
- Include details: what you tried, screenshots, error messages.
- Got it solved? Mark the best reply ✅ — future-you (and others) will thank you.

## 🛡 The vibe
Be kind, no spam, no piracy/malware, no NSFW, keep it legal. Full rules: [/rules](/rules). Mods are here to keep the place awesome, not to boss you around.
