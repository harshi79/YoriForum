# 🛡 Join the YoriForum team — staff recruitment (OPEN)

*Post as a thread in 📢 Announcements. Keep it open; review applications monthly.*

Want to help run the fastest-growing corner of the internet? ⚡ We're recruiting volunteer staff. No pay (we're a $0 community!) — but serious status, power, and experience.

## Roles
- **🛡 Moderator** — assigned sections: approve posts, lock/merge/split, warn/suspend, kill spam. The shield wall.
- **📌 Curator** — feature great content (FrontPage), organize sections, maintain indexes/pinned lists.
- **🙋 Support Helper** — patrol Help sections, answer + mark solved, guide newbies. (Best first staff role!)
- **🎉 Event Host** — run contests/giveaways from the contest kit.

## Requirements
- Regular rank+ (200 pts), 30+ days on the forum, clean record (no suspensions)
- Active most days; reachable on Telegram/Discord staff chat
- Helpful history: Best Answers, quality shares, or great reports
- 16+ · Good judgment under trolling fire 😅

## How to apply (reply to this thread)
```

```

## Process
1. Staff reviews monthly (deliberation is private).
2. Shortlisted → **2-week trial** with limited powers + a mentor.
3. Pass → confirmed staff (badge + color + Lounge). Struggle → kind release, reapply in 60 days.

## Staff code (break it = lose the shield)
Lead by example · No power trips, no favoritism, no leaking staff-room talk · Warn before punishing (except red lines) · Report conflicts of interest (friends' flags → another mod handles) · Stay active or step down gracefully.

*Being staff = community-management experience for your CV, a network, and a forum that loves you. Apply below 👇*
