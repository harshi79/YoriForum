# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

## What is YoriForum?
A free community forum for tech, help, and hanging out — PHCorner-style. Ask questions, share what you build, learn stuff, meet people.

## How do I join?
Hit **Sign Up** and register with email — or one-click login with GitHub/Google. It's free forever.

## Why is my first post "pending approval"?
Anti-spam shield 🛡. A mod approves first posts from new members, usually within a few hours. After that, you post freely.

## How do ranks work?
You earn **points** from posting and getting upvotes/reactions. Points unlock ranks automatically: 🌱 Newbie → 👤 Member → 💬 Regular → 🔥 Veteran → 💎 Elite → 👑 Legend. Full ladder is in the welcome thread. No grinding alts — cheaters get reset.

## How do I post images or files?
Use the upload button in the editor (max a few MB per file). Images, screenshots, zips and docs welcome. Don't upload anything illegal or NSFW.

## How do I mark a question solved?
In **Help & Support**, the thread author (or a mod) can mark the best reply as ✅ Best Answer. Please do this — it helps the next person with the same problem.

## Is there a dark mode?
Yes 🌙 — click your avatar → Night Mode. Your eyes at 2 AM thank us.

## How do I report a post or user?
Click the **flag icon** under any post, pick a reason, done. Mods review every flag. For private matters use the Contact page.

## Can I promote my YouTube/channel/product?
Genuine sharing in Showcase is fine. Drive-by ads, referral spam, and link-dumping are not — that's what the spam filter is for.

## I forgot my password / can't login
Use **Forgot Password** on the login screen. Logged in via GitHub/Google? Use that same button — not the password field.

## How do I delete my account or data?
Contact the staff via the Contact page from your registered email. We'll delete or anonymize your account.

## Who runs this place?
Admins (⚡) + Mods (🛡). They're volunteers — be nice to them. Want to help mod one day? Be an awesome member first.
